Don is a co-founder and part of the engineering team at Fynbos.

He believes the primary goal at Fynbos is to reduce the friction around making
payments.

> We want to build technology that is simple and secure by default.

Don's previous work included web and mobile app development for a sports
tracking company. After some time he joined the team at
[Coil](https://coil.com/) where he was first introduced to payment systems
through his work on [Interledger](https://interledger.org/), and later through
[Mojaloop](https://mojaloop.io/). Don also had the pleasure of working with the
[TigerBeetle](https://tigerbeetle.com/) team on the first ProtoBeetle and was
the initial developer of their Go client.

During this time he had to wrangle (and became something of an expert) with
[AWS](https://aws.amazon.com), [Kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/) and
[Vault](https://www.vaultproject.io/). He spent quite a bit of time grappling
with typescript and the node ecosystem, until finally he transitioned to Go
where he hasn't looked back :)

Don studied mechanical engineering at the
[University of Cape Town (UCT)](https://www.uct.ac.za). He was part of the team
that launched a single stage water and air propelled rocket - setting the world
record in that category at 830 metres high.
